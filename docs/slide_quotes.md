# Slide quotes: colorful body-size language

Source: NFL.com-style scouting report excerpts, masked (`[PLAYER]`, `[SCHOOL]`). Attribute on slides as "scouting report excerpt, item ID." Quotes are verbatim, with the broken apostrophes in the source fixed.

Keep the jokes aimed at the analysts' wording, not at the players. Don't use school names, teams, or anything else that could identify a prospect.
